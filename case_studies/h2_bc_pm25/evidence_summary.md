# H2 Evidence Summary: Black Carbon Feedback and PM2.5

H2 examines a wintertime black-carbon absorbing-feedback hypothesis for the Guanzhong Basin.

The curated branch results capture an ARI-related shortwave-radiation response, but they do not capture a complete evidence chain from BC-load perturbation to PM2.5 response. The high-BC branches are nearly indistinguishable from their normal-BC counterparts in the available diagnostics, and vertical absorptive-heating diagnostics are not included. The manuscript therefore does not treat the BC mechanism as physically disproven; instead, it reports that the present branch set fails to express the BC perturbation magnitude, vertical structure, optical effect, and heating pathway needed to support the hypothesized dome-like feedback chain.
