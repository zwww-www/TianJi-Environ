# H1 Evidence Summary: ARI and Ozone Response

H1 examines whether aerosol--radiation interaction modulates the ozone response to an approximately 30% NOx-reduction premise over the North China Plain summertime case.

The curated branch results show directionally consistent responses in shortwave radiation and boundary-layer height, indicating that ARI-related radiative and mixing signals are expressed. However, the MDA8 ozone response is weak, and key optical or photolysis diagnostics such as AOD550 and JNO2 are unavailable in the current evidence package. The manuscript therefore treats H1 as partially supported rather than as a closed ozone-control mechanism.
