# Third-Party Data and Model Assets

The repository does not include raw WRF-Chem input/output files, meteorological forcing data, emissions inventories, reanalysis products, satellite products, or observational datasets that may be subject to external licenses or access restrictions.

Where such data are needed for full reproduction, users should obtain them from the original providers and follow the relevant terms of use. The included artifacts are derived summaries, source tables, and diagnostic products prepared for manuscript-level verification.
